# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asian
markets, the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it's too late, and the US lead
over China at a record low.

## The day in one image

Three big hero scenes lead the front page:

- **AI rout hits Asia** — the Kospi fell 3.3% to 4,708.54, shedding 161 points
  (intraday −5.8%); Samsung −3.1%, SK Hynix −2.5%.
- **Guterres: regulate AI before it's too late** — 150 delegates back a risk
  framework at the Bridgetown summit in Barbados; a blueprint, not a treaty.
- **US lead over China at record low** — Asia Power Index 2026: US 78.9,
  China 73.6, the gap of 5.3 the narrowest since 2018; India third at 40.5.

Below them, ten more stories, a world map with numbered pins, an 18-item
"Also on Monday" list, and a red DATE CHECK box that keeps out stories from
other days (Democrats reach 258, the Sparta II fire, El Chapo Isidro captured,
the EU–Indonesia pact, the Nigeria boat, the ICJ Myanmar ruling).

## How to run

```sh
python3 draw.py            # regenerates entry.png (1024x1024)
python3 main.py            # verifies entry.png and copies it to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library. Scenes are drawn
illustrations, not photographs. Chips: REPORTED = confirmed by wires;
CLAIM = one side's claim, independently unconfirmed.
